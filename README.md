## 📖 About

CaféConnect is a full-stack web application that modernizes the café ordering experience through a self-ordering system.

Customers can browse the menu, view ingredients and allergen information, place orders without creating an account, track order status, and provide feedback after pickup. Staff manage payments, kitchen preparation, and order completion through dedicated dashboards.

---

## ✨ Features

### Customer
- Browse menu by category
- View product details & allergens
- Add items to cart
- Anonymous ordering
- Live order tracking
- Customer feedback

### Staff
- Cashier payment management
- Kitchen order workflow
- Admin dashboard
- Product & category management
- Promotions management
- Sales & feedback overview
